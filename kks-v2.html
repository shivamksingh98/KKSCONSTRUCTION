About The Company

Built on Engineering Integrity Since Founding

KKS Construction — Kundan Kumar Singh Construction — is a government-focused civil engineering company headquartered at Karnibag, Deoghar, Jharkhand. We specialize in executing public works and government-tendered infrastructure with an unwavering commitment to technical quality and timely delivery.

From water tanks and check dams to PWD highways and government buildings — every contract is backed by experienced engineers, certified equipment, and field teams trained in government-standard construction protocols across all climate and terrain types in Jharkhand.

Mission

To deliver government-standard infrastructure that serves communities for generations, through disciplined execution, quality materials, and engineering excellence.

Vision

To become the most trusted government infrastructure contractor in Jharkhand — recognized for integrity, execution capability, and enduring quality.

Engineering Services

Water Tank Construction

02

PCC Road Construction

Plain cement concrete village connectivity and municipal roads. Proper sub-base, DLC, expansion joints, M25–M30 grade PCC per IRC specifications. PMGSY and MGNREGS compliant.

PMGSY · MGNREGS
03

PWD Roads & Highways

State highway and district road construction under PWD contracts — flexible bituminous and rigid pavement with drainage infrastructure, MoRT&H specifications, DPR-based execution.

PWD Jharkhand
04

Check Dams & Weirs

Masonry and RCC check dams, nala bunding, ogee weir spillways for water conservation and groundwater recharge. CWC guideline-compliant structural design for flood and flow conditions.

DRDA · IWMP
05

Water Resource Projects

Canal lining, irrigation channel construction, lift irrigation, watershed management structures. Executed under state WRD and minor irrigation schemes to hydrological design standards.

WRD · Minor Irrigation Dept.
06

Building Construction

Government office buildings, school blocks, health sub-centres, community halls, and residential complexes. Full project lifecycle — structural frame through MEP, finishing, BIS code compliance.

JBCCL · Education Dept.
